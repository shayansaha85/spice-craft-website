OUR HERITAGE & PASSION

More than just dining.

crafted with soul.

🌿 Farm Fresh Ingredients 🔥 Tandoor & Wok Mastery 🍸 Handcrafted Mixology

Tucked along Hazra Road in Ballygunge, Spice Kraft is built on a passion for honest, inventive gastronomy. We bring food lovers together through dishes that burst with color, aroma, and personality.

Our chefs blend authentic Continental cooking techniques, slow-braised Kashmiri Mughlai curries, vibrant East Asian street traditions, and fresh coastal seafood into plates designed to delight the palate.

Whether dropping by for a sunlit family lunch, a celebratory feast, or an unhurried dinner paired with signature cocktails, our doors open with warm smiles and thoughtful service.
